# Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

SkillsMate is made by HomieLab.com. This policy covers the SkillsMate app for macOS, Linux and Windows.

## The short version

SkillsMate doesn't collect, store or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your computer

SkillsMate reads and changes the skill folders your coding agents use, such as `~/.claude/skills` and `~/.agents/skills`, and the skills lockfile of the `skills` command-line tool. Backups of changed skills are kept on your computer, in SkillsMate's own data folder. None of this leaves your computer unless you use one of the online features below.

In the Mac App Store version, macOS asks you to choose your home folder once. SkillsMate only uses that access to read and change skill folders and the skills lockfile.

## What is sent over the internet

SkillsMate contacts these services only when you use the feature that needs them:

- **skills.sh** (search, Discover, Official, Topics, installing and updating skills): your search terms, and the names and sources of skills you install or update.
- **skills.sh audits and update checks** (only when you click Check): the names and sources of your installed skills.
- **GitHub** (downloadable versions only, when you check for app updates): a request for the latest SkillsMate release.

These requests include no personal identifiers. The services may log your IP address as part of normal web traffic; see their own privacy policies.

## Children

SkillsMate is a developer tool and isn't directed at children.

## Changes

We'll update this page if the app's behavior changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
